PRACTICAL 13 - INSTALLATION OF METAMASK AND STUDY OF ETHER SPENDING PER TRANSACTION
======================================================================================

AIM
Install the MetaMask wallet and understand how Ether is spent as a transaction fee.

REQUIREMENTS
- A supported web browser such as Chrome or Firefox.
- Internet connection.
- Official MetaMask website: https://metamask.io/
- For practice, use a test network and test ETH. Do not use real funds for a classroom demo.

WHAT IS METAMASK?
MetaMask is a wallet application/browser extension that manages blockchain accounts
and lets a user connect to decentralized applications (dApps). It can be used to
approve transactions and interact with smart contracts.

INSTALLATION STEPS
1. Open the official website https://metamask.io/.
2. Choose the official browser extension for your browser.
3. Install it from the linked official browser store.
4. Open the MetaMask extension.
5. Create a new wallet for practice or import an existing wallet only when appropriate.
6. Set a strong wallet password.
7. If using a Secret Recovery Phrase (SRP), write it down offline and store it securely.
8. Never share your SRP or private key with anyone. A legitimate support person does
   not need your recovery phrase.
9. Open the wallet and note your public account address. It is safe to share an
   address when someone needs to send you funds, but it does not reveal your private key.

STUDYING AN ETHER TRANSACTION FEE
1. Use a test network such as Sepolia for learning.
2. Obtain test ETH only from a trusted faucet for that test network.
3. Connect the wallet to the relevant dApp or Remix contract environment.
4. Initiate a test transaction and review the fee estimate in the wallet.
5. After the transaction is submitted, open its Activity/details view.
6. Record the transaction hash, gas used, effective gas price and total fee.
7. Open the transaction in the relevant block explorer to inspect the on-chain details.

GAS AND TRANSACTION FEE
- Gas is the unit that measures computational work performed by the EVM.
- Gas used is the actual amount of gas consumed by the executed transaction.
- Gas limit is the maximum amount of gas the sender permits the transaction to use.
- Gas price is the cost per unit of gas.
- Gwei is a denomination of Ether: 1 ETH = 1,000,000,000 Gwei.
- On an EIP-1559 Ethereum transaction, the effective gas price reflects the base fee
  and the priority fee, subject to the transaction's fee settings.

Basic fee calculation:
Transaction fee = Gas used x Effective gas price

Example:
Gas used = 21,000
Effective gas price = 20 Gwei
Fee = 21,000 x 20 Gwei = 420,000 Gwei = 0.00042 ETH

The example is only an arithmetic illustration. Actual gas used and gas price vary
by transaction type and network congestion. A smart-contract call usually consumes
more gas than a simple ETH transfer.

IMPORTANT DIFFERENCES
- Transaction value: the amount of ETH being transferred or sent to a payable function.
- Gas fee: the fee paid to validators for processing the transaction.
- Gas limit: a cap on gas consumption, not the amount of ETH sent to the contract.
- A failed on-chain transaction can still consume gas.
