PRACTICAL 17 - VIVA QUESTIONS AND ANSWERS
=======================================================

1. Q: What is a blockchain?
   A: A distributed ledger in which records are linked using cryptographic hashes and agreed by network participants.

2. Q: What is a public blockchain?
   A: A blockchain generally open for anyone to read and participate according to its protocol rules.

3. Q: What is a private blockchain?
   A: A permissioned blockchain controlled by one organization.

4. Q: What is a consortium blockchain?
   A: A permissioned blockchain governed by a group of organizations.

5. Q: What is a hybrid blockchain?
   A: A design combining private/permissioned components with selected public blockchain features.

6. Q: Give two public blockchain examples.
   A: Bitcoin and Ethereum.

7. Q: How can blockchain be used in supply chains?
   A: It can record provenance, custody changes and shipment milestones shared across participants.

8. Q: How can blockchain be used in healthcare?
   A: It can support audit trails and proofs of data integrity; sensitive medical data needs privacy protection.

9. Q: What is one advantage of blockchain?
   A: It can provide shared, tamper-evident records and traceability across parties.

10. Q: What is one limitation of blockchain?
   A: Scalability, transaction cost, privacy and smart-contract security can be challenging.
